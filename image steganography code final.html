Welcome to Image Steganography

How It Works

Image steganography is the process of hiding secret messages inside an image without significantly altering its appearance. This ensures secure communication while keeping the hidden data undetectable.

How to Encode a Message

Step 1: Select an Image

Click the "Select Image" button or drag and drop an image into the designated area.

Step 2: Enter Your Secret Message

Type the message you want to hide in the provided text area.

Step 3: Add an Encryption Key

Enter a secure encryption key for extra protection.

Step 4: Click "Encode"

Click the "Encode" button to hide the message. You can download the modified image.

×

How to Decode a Message

Step 1: Upload the Encoded Image

Click the "Select Image" button or drag and drop the encoded image.

Step 2: Enter the Decryption Key

Enter the same encryption key used during encoding.

Step 3: Click "Decode"

Click the "Decode" button to extract the hidden message.

Step 4: View the Hidden Message

After decryption, the hidden message will be displayed in the text area.
